# Trends page (index.html)

University R&D spending from FY {first_year} to FY {last_year}, nationally or split by group.

## Controls

- **Measure**: what to chart, for example All R&D, Federal government, a federal agency, or a field of research.
- **Split by**:
  - Nothing (national total)
  - Institution type (Carnegie 2021, top 8)
  - Public or private
  - State (top 8, rest combined)
  - Minority-serving institution
  - HBCU
  - Has a medical school
  - Land-grant
- **Show as**:
  - Amount: dollars (or people for personnel measures).
  - Index (FY {first_year} = 100): growth relative to FY {first_year}, so groups of different size can be compared.
  - Share of national total: each group's percent of the national figure.
- **Show projection to FY {projection_last_year}**: adds projected values with shaded uncertainty ranges. Not available for Share of national total. Measures without projections (such as personnel) show a note explaining why. See projections.md.

## Workload up, resources down

A second section charts COGR's count of federal research requirements since 1991 next to the national share of indirect costs recovered per dollar of direct research costs. Recovery figures are national only (NCSES Table 12, standard-form institutions); NCSES does not release them by institution.

## Tips

- Hover a chart to see values for every line in a year.
- The chart toolbar (top right of each chart, on hover) can download the chart as a PNG and zoom.
- For a specific institution, use Compare. For any other slice or a table of numbers, use Explore.
