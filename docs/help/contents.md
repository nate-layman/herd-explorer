# HERD Explorer help: contents

Help documents the Ask page can read when someone asks how to use the site or how it was made. Each line is one document: its file name, then what it covers.

- [site-overview.md](site-overview.md): The pages of the site and what each is for; reporting a data error; the disclaimer; who made it and how it is funded.
- [trends.md](trends.md): The Trends page: national and group trends, the Split by and Show as options, the projection switch, the workload and indirect cost charts.
- [compare.md](compare.md): The Compare page: adding and removing institutions, measures, per-faculty and index views, group medians for context, projections, sharing a link.
- [pivot-tables.md](pivot-tables.md): Building pivot tables on Explore's Table tab: quick views, the Rows / then by / Columns / Measure dropdowns, nesting and subtotals, More settings, filtering, CSV export, worked examples.
- [chart-builder.md](chart-builder.md): Explore's Chart builder tab: example charts, Start your own, putting fields on axes, color and filters, mark types, multiple chart tabs.
- [animate.md](animate.md): Explore's Animate tab: bar races, line races, moving scatter plots and maps that play through the years; the example buttons, the Show, Measure, How many, filter and speed controls, and what the motion means.
- [data-sources.md](data-sources.md): The About HERD page: what the HERD Survey is, who reports, what counts as R&D, what it asks, timing, history, caveats such as double-counted subawards; where the data come from, survey years and forms, units, how the data were checked against NCSES, what NCSES does not release by institution, institution types, imputed values, downloads; what is HERD data and what is not; ranks; the other sources (Carnegie, Census population, BEA prices and state GDP, EPSCoR, IPEDS faculty, enrollment, doctorates, spending and endowment, GSS graduate students).
- [projections.md](projections.md): What the projections are and are not, what is projected, how to read the shaded ranges, limitations, where to download them.
- [projection-model.md](projection-model.md): The projection model in detail: the growth model, random effects and shared shocks, size-dependent noise, simulation, backtesting and scores, the R code, how to reproduce it.
- [ask.md](ask.md): How the Ask page works: API keys and providers, how queries run, what is sent to the AI provider, limits.
