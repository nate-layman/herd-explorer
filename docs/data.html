Data & sources

How the data were put together

  • Years. FY 2012 to FY 2024. All of these years use the same survey design, introduced in FY 2010, and include the short-form survey for institutions with less than $1 million in R&D, so national totals are complete.
  • Checked against NCSES. Each build compares 195 national totals with the tables NCSES publishes (NSF 26-304, Tables 2 and 12). All match exactly; the build stops if any do not.
  • Two survey forms. Institutions with $1 million or more in R&D answer the standard form; smaller ones answer a short form with fewer questions. Measures available only on the standard form are labeled that way below, and their national totals leave out short-form institutions (about 0.1 percent of R&D).
  • Not released by institution. NCSES does not publish, for individual institutions, how institution funds split into institutionally financed research, cost sharing and unrecovered indirect costs, or how much indirect cost was recovered. Those appear on this site only as national totals from the published tables.
  • Personnel counts are released from FY 2022 onward.
  • Institution type comes from the 2021 Carnegie Classification, held fixed for every year so a group means the same institutions throughout. HERD reporting units without a usable IPEDS ID (combined campuses, system offices, research institutes) are matched by a hand-checked crosswalk. Seven independent research institutes and three system offices, which Carnegie does not classify, are labeled by HERD Explorer.
  • Imputed values. NCSES imputes some values for nonresponse; the survey-values download keeps NCSES's status code (i = imputed, n = not available) for every value.
  • Dollars are nominal (not adjusted for inflation).
