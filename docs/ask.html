Ask the data

Ask a question in plain language. An AI model writes a database query, the query runs here in your browser on the HERD data (FY 2012 to FY 2024), and the model explains the result. You can always open the query to see exactly what was counted. AI can misread a question or write the wrong query, so check important numbers on the Trends or Compare pages.

No key yet. Your key stays in this browser tab and goes only to the provider it belongs to.

Answers come from NCSES HERD Survey public microdata, FY 2012-2024, nominal dollars, with institution type from the 2021 Carnegie Classification. Queries are read-only and run in your browser; only your question and the query results are sent to the AI provider you chose.
