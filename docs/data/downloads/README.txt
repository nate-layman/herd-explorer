HERD Explorer data download

Source: NCSES Higher Education Research and Development (HERD) Survey public microdata, FY 2012-2024,
https://ncses.nsf.gov/explore-data/microdata/higher-education-research-development
Institution type (sector): Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education, 2021 update.
Dollar values are in whole nominal dollars. NCSES publishes HERD in thousands of dollars; HERD Explorer multiplies
by 1,000, so $1,234 thousand in an NCSES table is $1,234,000 here. Personnel values are people or full-time equivalents.
Measure definitions: measure_catalog sheet, or https://nate-layman.github.io/herd-explorer/data.html
Built on 2026-10-28 by HERD Explorer, https://github.com/nate-layman/herd-explorer
